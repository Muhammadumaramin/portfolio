About

Muhammad Umar Amin.
Commerce meets mathematics.

E-commerce manager, marketplace operations professional, and quantitative analyst based in San Antonio, Texas.

Turning complexity
into a clear next step.

I’m Umar—an e-commerce manager who enjoys bringing structure to complex business problems. My path connects marketplace operations, mathematics, analytics, and teaching.

My e-commerce background began at Ahmad Traders in Pakistan, where I worked across marketplace listings, inventory, customer service, and fulfillment. I later moved to the United States for graduate study at Lamar University, completing my M.S. in Computational & Quantitative Methods in December 2025 with a 4.00 GPA. Teaching mathematics and interning in analytics strengthened both my technical foundation and the way I communicate. Today, I lead an e-commerce team at Haifa Wholesale in San Antonio.

What interests me most is the connection between the numbers and what happens next: which product needs attention, where an order workflow breaks down, or what a team should prioritize. I value clear communication, careful analysis, and taking responsibility for follow-through. Outside work, I enjoy painting, calligraphy, gardening, and community events.
